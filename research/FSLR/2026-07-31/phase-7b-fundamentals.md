## Summary

**The business is high quality and cheap; the insiders are selling and the
cash flow is negative. That combination yields CAUTION, not CONFIRM.**

On the P&L, FSLR is the strongest name in its industry. **P/E 13.01, forward
P/E 8.87, PEG 0.33** — against a Technology sector at P/E 34.31 and a solar
peer group where the next-largest name (NXT) trades at 23.27. **ROE 18.51%,
ROIC 16.69%, operating margin 34.98%, and Debt/Equity of 0.02** — essentially
debt-free, with **$237.2M of long-term debt against $9.88B of equity**.
Revenue growth is **+27.3% TTM** and EPS growth **+31.57% TTM**. Two
consecutive verified beats: **Q2 2026 $3.92 vs $2.86 (+37.1%)** and **Q1 2026
$3.22 vs $2.87 (+12.1%)**, with full-year guidance reaffirmed both times.

Two things contradict the picture, and one of them is decisive.

**Insiders sold in a cluster the day before the print.** On **2026-07-29** —
one session before Q2 earnings — **five distinct C-suite officers sold**: the
**CEO** (Newcomer, 50,000 sh), **CFO** (Melwani, 381,288 sh against a 395,478
option exercise), **COO** (Foo, 10,000), **CTO** (Rasmussen, 261,301) and
**General Counsel** (Mulligan, 18,213). A 10% holder (Thrivent) sold on both
07-29 and 07-31. Finnhub MSPR has been **negative for four consecutive
months** (Mar −14.90, Apr −100.00, May −65.90, Jun −18.78), with two months
beyond the |30| threshold.

**Operating cash flow is negative.** The Q1 2026 10-Q reports **net cash used
in operating activities of −$214,866,000** with capex of $118,529,000 — free
cash flow of roughly **−$333M in a quarter that earned $346.6M of net
income.** Cash fell from **$2.4B at YE-2025 to $2.36B at Q1 to $1.7B at Q2**.
The 45X credits phase 6 identified as a ~$2.1B annual tailwind are being
**booked as income well ahead of being monetized in cash.**

**Verdict: `fundamental_signal = BULLISH`, `tier_adjustment = CAUTION`,
`contradiction_count = 1`.** The earnings and growth/margin axes confirm the
