- **Direction call: RIGHT** — 11.85 → 15.05, +27.0%. Both targets reached (T1 07-15, T2 08-03).

  flow led the price by ~3 weeks while the options tape stayed net-bearish the entire time.
- **Pattern call: NA — and correctly so.** All six detectors returned `detected:false`, the plan explicitly refused to
  force a pattern onto noise, and at review the engine still reports no pattern. The restraint was right; nothing to tune.
- **Level quality: STOP_TOO_TIGHT.** MAE 1.20R against MFE 0.60R. The hard stop sat 0.52 below entry — 0.80× the
  ATR14 of 0.65 — in a name that then printed a 1.64 single-day range (2.5× ATR) on 07-22. More damning than the

  placed at 11.28, inside that gap. The floor the plan identified ($10 put wall, $11 pin) is exactly where price
  actually turned — 10.16. The analysis found the right floor and then entered 1.60 above it.
- **Sizing review: UNDERSIZED.** Starter 1.0% was defensible ex-ante and did limit the damage, but it was cut one
  step by a debate steelman that the plan's *own falsifiable tests* went on to disprove. The framework behaved

## 5. Lessons → reasoning ledger

- **L-0005** (CANDIDATE, scope `setup:dark-pool-shelf`, `sentiment:crowded-short`, `regime:transitional`, `ticker:PATH`):
  In a name with >25% short float, express a dark-pool-shelf long as **defined risk**, not a stopped stock long. High-SI
  names resolve by violent two-way flushes that harvest stops before the thesis pays; a defined-risk structure has no
  stop to harvest and survives the round trip.
- **L-0006** (CANDIDATE, scope `risk:air-pocket`, `setup:dark-pool-shelf`, `gate:invalidation`):
  Never place a stock stop inside an air pocket you named in `reasons_against`. If a reason says "no support between X
  and Y", the stop belongs below Y — or the trade must be defined-risk. Cross-check every stop against the
  `reasons_against` list before publishing.
- **L-0007** (CANDIDATE, scope `gate:debate-disconfirmed`, `gate:sizing`):
  When a debate steelman carries an explicit falsifiable test that has **not yet fired**, treat the
  debate-disconfirmed size cut as **provisional**. Record the test and a restore condition in the plan, and restore the
  size step once the test comes back clean.

Also recorded in the ledger as new supporting evidence: **L-0001** (its volume-confirmed-close add trigger would have
re-entered on the 07-29 close at 12.59 and captured +19.5% to 15.05) and **L-0002** (see the companion eval of the
2026-06-21 PATH plan, whose watch-only stance avoided a short into a +46.5% rally).

## 6. Proposed rubric edits (propose-only — for the user to accept)

1. **`.claude/skills/stock-deep-dive/rubrics/sizing-rubric.md`** (debate-disconfirmation gate, ~lines 137-140) —
   add: if the bear's residual rests on a claim with an explicit falsifiable test that has not yet resolved, mark the
   size cut **PROVISIONAL** and emit a `restore_condition`. *Rationale:* the PATH steelman named two tests (a
   13D/13G/S-3/424B filing, or large-tier buy_ratio < 0.45); neither ever fired, yet the cut to starter 1.0% held
   through a +27% move.
2. **`.claude/skills/trade-plan/phases/phase-A4-plans.md`** — add an **expression-selection step before sizing**: when
   short interest > 25% of float, or the plan's own `reasons_against` name an air pocket between entry and stop, prefer
   a defined-risk options expression and mark any stock expression as secondary / wider-stop-only. *Rationale:*
   identical thesis, opposite results by vehicle.
3. **`.claude/skills/stock-deep-dive/rubrics/invalidation-rubric.md`** — require **one primary stop convention**
   (close-basis or intraday), with the other explicitly marked secondary, and report R under the primary. *Rationale:*
   this plan resolves to −1.00R or −0.54R depending on which of its two stated stops governs.
4. **`.claude/skills/trade-plan/phases/phase-A4-plans.md`** — require a **post-T1 stop-management rule** on any
   multi-target stock plan (breakeven or 1-ATR trail). *Rationale:* with no trailing rule, the close-basis runner gave
   back +1.04R and then lost 2.12R on the untouched original stop.

## 7. One-paragraph takeaway

**The analysis was right about almost everything and the trade still lost, because the plan chose the one vehicle that
could be killed by the risk it had already identified in writing.** Every bull pillar held — the $644M block was real
accumulation (buy_ratio 0.672 at review, no offering ever filed), the 32% short float squeezed exactly as described
once 12.35 broke, and the named Maestro/AI catalyst arrived and carried the name +27% to 15.05. The two correct bear
points were both about *path*: a UBS downgrade into an AI-software selloff opened the −15.6% air pocket the plan itself
had mapped from 11.60 down to $10. The stock plan put its stop at 11.28, inside that mapped gap, and was taken out on a
10-cent wick one session before its first target traded. The two defined-risk structures, holding the identical thesis,
sat through the same flush and both reached maximum value. The next PATH plan — and the next plan on any name above 25%
short float — should read its own `reasons_against` as a **vehicle-selection instruction**: when you have written down
that there is no support for 1.60 beneath your entry, you have already proven you cannot use a stop there, and the
correct expression is defined risk.
