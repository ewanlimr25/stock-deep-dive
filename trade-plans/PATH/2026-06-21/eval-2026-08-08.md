# Trade-Plan Eval — PATH (plan 2026-06-21 → reviewed 2026-08-08)

> *For research and educational use only. Not financial advice.*

**Plan:** [trade-plans/PATH/2026-06-21/trade-plan.md](trade-plan.md) — NEUTRAL, conviction 0.55, horizon 1-4w, spot ref 10.27
**Trade taken:** **none — and that was the correct outcome.** The plan was explicitly watch-only at 0% size, with a
conditional short armed only by two consecutive daily closes below 10.00.
**Window:** 2026-06-22 → 2026-08-07 (33 sessions), well past the 21D review window for a 1-4w horizon.

---

## 1. Outcome (path-aware, ±1R)

- **Status: INCONCLUSIVE — trigger never armed.** `r_achieved` **0.00** (no position, 0% size, no risk taken).
- **The short trigger never fired.** Across 33 sessions PATH printed exactly **one** close below 10.00 (9.93); the plan
  required **two consecutive**. Minimum close in the entire window was 9.93.
- **The stand-down rule fired instead, and early.** The plan's kill-switch — "stand down if it reclaims $11" — was
  triggered on the **2026-07-01 close above 11.00**. Price never returned.
- **What was avoided:** PATH ran **10.27 → 15.05, +46.5%**, high 15.10. A short entered at the plan's 9.95 with its
  10.65 stop would have been stopped for −1R almost immediately and then watched the name rally another 41% beyond it.

This is a plan that made money by not existing. The rubric's INCONCLUSIVE label is technically right and materially
understates it: **a correctly-executed stand-down is the highest-value outcome an unfavourable setup can produce.**

## 2. Reasoning review (process, not just result)

- **Direction call: UNRESOLVED** — no directional position was ever taken, which is the honest grade. The *latent*
  bear tilt would have been badly wrong, and the plan's own machinery is what prevented it from being expressed.
- **Flow↔chart agreement call (DIVERGENT): RIGHT** — and decisively. Flow pointed up, chart pointed down, and flow
  won. Refusing to trade the divergence was the single correct decision here.
- **Pattern call: UNCONFIRMED** — the head-and-shoulders never confirmed. The 10.07 neckline was tested twice
  (06-18 9.88, 06-26 9.87) and defended both times, then became the base of the rally. The engine reports no H&S at review.
- **Level quality: GOOD** — the levels were well chosen even though the directional read was not. The $10
  neckline/gamma-flip really was the pivot; the plan simply guessed the wrong side of it, and its own confirmation
  requirement (two closes) protected it from that guess.
- **Sizing review: APPROPRIATE** — 0% until confirmation is exactly right for a DIVERGENT read, and is the reason
  this plan cost nothing.

## 6. Proposed rubric edits (propose-only — for the user to accept)

   non-confirmation of the bull case, and should trigger a re-check date rather than a conviction cut. *Rationale:* the
   0.48 here was an institution mid-build; 18 days later the same lane printed $644M at 1.000.
2. **`.claude/skills/stock-deep-dive/rubrics/sizing-rubric.md`** — set a **minimum-N floor** on `signal_backtest` win
   rates used for Kelly `p`: below N=20, blend toward the conviction-bin fallback rather than adopting the empirical
   rate outright, and state the blend in `deviation_reason`. *Rationale:* this plan used p=0.375 from n=8, whose
   confidence interval spans roughly 0.10–0.75 — it cannot distinguish a 37.5% edge from a 65% one.

## 7. One-paragraph takeaway

**The bear thesis was wrong in almost every particular, and the plan still cost nothing — because it refused to trade a
DIVERGENT read and demanded two confirming closes that never came.** The head-and-shoulders never confirmed, the $10
neckline held as support rather than breaking as a trigger, and the dark-pool lane that was scored as "refusing to
confirm" at 0.48 went on to print a $644M ask-side block three weeks later. Every directional inference was wrong and
none of it mattered, because the sizing discipline was right: 0% until a level breaks, plus an explicit
reclaim-above-$11 kill-switch that fired on 07-01 and shut the idea down before the +46.5% move. Read alongside the
2026-07-13 eval, the pair makes one point twice: on this name the **dark-pool lane was right both times and the
chart-pattern and fundamentals lanes were wrong both times** — and in each case the money was made or saved by the
*expression and sizing rules*, not by the directional call.
